Your Hermes agents, in your pocket.

Hermes Mobile connects to the Hermes Agent already running on your own Mac, Linux or Windows computer. Chat with every bot, talk to them hands-free, and help them past sign-in screens from anywhere. Nothing is hosted by us; your data stays on your machine.

What you get

Chat with every bot

See answers as they are written, and step in when a bot needs you.

  • Live streaming replies, reasoning and tool calls
  • Approve actions and answer the agent's questions
  • Group chats shared with Hermes on your desktop

Voice mode

Have a hands-free conversation with any bot. Replies are spoken sentence by sentence in that bot's own ElevenLabs voice, so you hear the start while the rest is still being written.

  • Everything is still transcribed into the chat
  • Pick each bot's voice in the app

Screen share for sign-ins

When a bot's browser hits a login, SSO, 2FA or captcha, you get an Open screen button. See the bot's browser live, tap and type into it, tap Done, and the bot carries on.

  • Works with a copy of your real Chrome profile for Google-protected sign-ins

How it works

Your phone talks straight to your computer.

There's no Hermes Mobile cloud in the middle. The installer adds a small plugin to the Hermes dashboard you already have and makes it reachable from your phone.

  1. Hermes Mobile The app on your iPhone or Android phone.
  2. Secure connection Tailscale (recommended), your home network, or your own HTTPS URL.
  3. Hermes dashboard On your computer, with the hermes-mobile plugin added.
  4. Your bots The same agents, tools, memory and files you already use.

Set it up in about five minutes.

Run one command on the computer that runs Hermes, then scan a QR code with your phone.
